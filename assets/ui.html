Hello, artisan

This UI is compiled ahead of time by artisanc into a mutable artisan::Node tree baked directly into the binary - nothing here is parsed at runtime. It's the same live tree the app can click, type into, and mutate below - one pipeline, not two.

A div is just a block-level container: it starts on its own line and adds margin above and below. A span, on the other hand, flows inline with no margin of its own - notice this text sits right after it on the same line.
